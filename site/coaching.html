Questions before you apply?

Can I join as a beginner?

Yes. Tell us where you’re starting from so we can assess what support makes sense for you.

What if my schedule changes every week?

Tell your coach when your availability changes. They can adjust your plan around the time you have to train.

Do I need a gym?

Tell us what equipment you have access to. We’ll discuss what setup makes sense for your goal on the consultation call.

How does my coach support me through the app?

You’ll find your plan in the app. Your coach reviews your check-ins, gives feedback and adjusts the plan as you progress.

What happens on the consultation call?

We’ll talk through your goals and routine, see whether coaching fits, and discuss your options and pricing.
